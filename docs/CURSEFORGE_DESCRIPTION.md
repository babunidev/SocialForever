# Social Forever — CurseForge description

Social Forever is a social-memory addon built exclusively for World of Warcraft: Forever.

It remembers the players you actually encounter while questing and exploring, keeps Nearby separate from the broader General-channel zone roster, and makes familiar names easier to recognize the next time your paths cross.

## Features

- Recently encountered players in a small movable Nearby list.
- Click a name to target that player.
- Deliberate, secure Invite buttons; Social Forever never sends invitations automatically.
- Account-wide encounter memory, including where and how often you have met someone.
- White, blue and purple familiarity colors for recurring players.
- Green, Yellow and Red personal marks.
- Private flags, custom flags and Kill on Sight reminders.
- Party ratings after a group ends.
- Optional same-mob grouping prompt that only offers a clickable Invite action.
- General-channel roster and yell support for the separate In this zone list.
- Nearby users can exchange Green, Yellow and Red marks through addon messages.

## Privacy

Flags, Kill on Sight, encounter counts, group history and declined-invite counts stay personal. Only color marks are shared with compatible nearby Social Forever users.

## Forever support

Built for World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1 / Interface 16001.

Version 0.4.7-beta passed automated Lua syntax, migration, privacy, mock behavior and package-integrity checks before release. Final launch-day in-game verification was deferred until the live client became playable.
